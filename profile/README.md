## Introduction

Safirum is a Swiss fintech company specialising in digital currency infrastructure, based in Rotkreuz, Canton Zug. With
CHF-S, Safirum issues a fully Swiss-franc-backed stablecoin on the Solana blockchain, designed primarily for institutional
applications. As a financial intermediary subject to the Swiss Anti-Money Laundering Act, Safirum is a member of VQF, a
self-regulatory organisation officially recognised by FINMA.

## CHF-S

CHF-S is a digital form of the Swiss franc for fast and cost-efficient transactions around the clock.

| | |
|---|---|
| Backing | Every CHF-S in circulation is backed 1:1 by Swiss francs held in segregated accounts at Swiss-based banks. |
| Identified users | Individuals pass a KYC check and companies a KYB check. A verified wallet is then approved for CHF-S, and CHF-S moves only between approved wallets. |
| Issuance | New CHF-S are minted on-chain only with the approval of four governors. |
| Network | Solana, on the Token-2022 standard. |
| Fee | 0.05% of the transaction amount, capped at 0.50 CHF-S, plus network fees. |
| Token | [`CHF2SFRvk7GhfxMQ76fcMBaGdjiP2iH3xF29yPZESdsk`](https://solscan.io/token/CHF2SFRvk7GhfxMQ76fcMBaGdjiP2iH3xF29yPZESdsk) |

### From Swiss francs to on-chain in four steps

1. Onboard. Complete the KYC/AML verification through our onboarding process.
2. Deposit CHF. Transfer Swiss francs to your regulated account.
3. Mint CHF-S. The equivalent amount of CHF-S is minted on-chain.
4. Transact. Use your on-chain Swiss francs for settlements and payments.

### Built for real-world applications

- Hedging and treasury: protect treasury positions against USD and EUR volatility with a franc that settles on-chain.
- B2B payments: settle invoices between companies at once, with fewer intermediaries.
- Cross-border commerce: let international customers pay Swiss businesses in CHF without FX conversion or banking delays.
- Tokenised real-world assets: a CHF settlement layer for tokenised bonds, real estate and securities.
